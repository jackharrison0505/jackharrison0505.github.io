How we work

Strategists with skin in the game.

We do the thinking ourselves and never outsource it. We never do the executing ourselves — we orchestrate the specialists who do, and we answer for the result. That distinction is the whole model.

Three engines

Simple. Clear. Easy to own.

The Strategy Sprint

Founder-led advisory that gets a brand ready for China — or tells it honestly not to go. We begin with a forensic business consultation: a mirror held up to your commercial reality, extracting the truths about the model that are hard to see from inside. Then market calibration — who to chase, what they value, when and how they buy — and a plan built around the answer.

What you get: a live commercial blueprint; a target list of the partners, channels and accounts that matter, each with an entry route; a 90-day execution calendar; a budget over a multi-year horizon; and a clear go / no-go recommendation we will stand behind.

The founder's role: leads the two-day Strategy War Room in person. 10–14 weeks.

Build & Operate

Access, then operate. First we build what the strategy needs: Chinese name and trademark protection; the right legal shape — entity, cross-border, or partner; WeChat Official Account, Channel and Mini Program; RED; e-commerce storefronts; PR and KOL; the trade and travel-agent network; payments and compliance. Then we run it — through a curated network of specialists we have vetted, contracted and hold to account, under one plan and one brand voice.

What you get: one point of contact. One P&L, reported to you monthly with complete transparency. Ongoing founder access and a strategic governance cadence, so the plan keeps being a plan rather than a memory.

The founder's role: opens the doors, chairs the governance, owns the outcome. Twelve months and beyond.

The Venture

For brands that want a partner, not a vendor. Some brands have the product and the story but not the appetite for another supplier invoice. For a select few we take a position in the China business alongside a modest fee — and become invested in the outcome. We design the structure, choose and contract the local operators who do the selling and shipping, and sit on the board to keep everyone honest.

We are the architect and the guardian, never the builder. The warehouses, the livestreams and the customer tickets belong to specialists whose whole business they are. Our job is to make sure they are the right ones, working to your plan.

Selective by design: one or two a year, with proper diligence on both sides.

How we deliver

Strategy in-house. Execution orchestrated.

Why don't we execute ourselves? Because execution in China is specialist work, and no small firm is expert at all of it. We would rather select the best operator for each job than be a mediocre one for every job.

So we run a curated network of vetted partners — managed on your behalf. We own the relationship, the quality and the P&L. You get one contact and one brand voice, and you never have to work out whose fault something is.

Principles

How we behave.

Founder-led, always

You deal with the people whose names are on the door. Both founders speak business-level Mandarin and have run consumer businesses in the region for decades.

Select, not scale

We work with a handful of brands at a time, deeply. If we can't give you real founder attention, we'll say so and introduce you to someone who can.

We govern, we don't execute

Every specialist we appoint is vetted, contracted and reviewed against your plan. If one underperforms, replacing them is our problem, not yours.

Evidence before spend

Every engagement begins with analysis and a multi-year forecast. If the numbers don't work, we tell you before you commit a yuan.

Participation where it fits

Where a brand and a business case merit it, we would rather take a stake than an invoice. Partners talk to each other differently from suppliers.

Complete transparency

You see everything we see — performance, costs, partners and problems — reported as they happen, not when they're tidy.

AI visibility

How AI describes you now shapes what people buy.

Doubao, DeepSeek, Kimi, Yuanbao and Tongyi Qianwen now write the consumer's shortlist. Hallucinations create false brand facts; competitors seed their own versions; traditional monitoring tools don't see any of it.

AI Visibility Audit — a one-off, structured read of how China's AI platforms describe your brand, your category and your competitors, with the gaps and risks ranked. AI Visibility Monitoring — ongoing tracking, alerts and the interpretation layer that turns changes into actions. Delivered with a specialist platform partner; owned and interpreted by us.
