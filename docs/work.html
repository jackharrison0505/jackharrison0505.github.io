Luxury hospitality · payments & compliance

Mandarin Oriental

A fully compliant China gift-card programme.

Selling gift cards in China — purchasable locally, redeemable across branded hotels including non-wholly-owned properties — means regulated payments, a dedicated entity and a great deal of paperwork. We established the entity, coordinated with the authorities, built the technology and run the programme and its B2B channel.

Access: WFOE set-up, legal & compliance, technical build.
Operate: programme management, finance, B2B channel growth.
