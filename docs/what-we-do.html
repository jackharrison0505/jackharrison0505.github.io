What we do

We launch brands in the East — and run them there.

Most consultancies hand you a report. We build the plan, launch the brand, and take operational responsibility for it in market. One partner, from first analysis to day-to-day trading.

Market entry strategy

Before anything is signed, we answer the hard questions. Is there demand for this brand in China, at what price, through which channels, against which competitors? We produce an in-depth market analysis and a highly detailed business development and market entry plan, with a budget forecast over a four-year horizon.

Deliverables: market analysis · positioning and pricing · channel strategy · entry plan · four-year P&L forecast

Brand launch

We set the brand up properly in market: Chinese name and trademark registration, corporate establishment and regulatory compliance, e-commerce storefronts (Tmall, JD, Douyin), retail and wholesale partners, and the marketing, PR and social presence a launch needs.

Deliverables: naming & trademark · entity set-up · e-commerce and retail · launch marketing & PR

Post-launch management & operational control

After launch we stay. Depending on what you need, that ranges from ongoing service support to full operational control — we run operations, HR, finance, marketing and tech for the brand in market, and report back with complete transparency. You keep your brand; we carry the operation.

Deliverables: operations · HR · finance · marketing · tech · reporting

Who we work with

Premium consumer, hospitality and lifestyle brands

We work with a select portfolio of brands that have a great story to tell — food and drink, watches and jewellery, fashion and activewear, beauty, hospitality.

Our track record includes Fortnum & Mason, Bremont and Charbonnel et Walker in Greater China, alongside Aman, Double Dutch and Varley. Before ArcOn, our founders launched the Jamie Oliver Restaurant Group across Asia and ran a 125-store KFC portfolio in Taiwan.
