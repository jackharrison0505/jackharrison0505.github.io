Who we are

Your strategic partner in China — from first access to daily operation.

ArcOn Brands is a founder-led partner for established consumer brands growing through China. We write the strategy. We set the brand up in market. And then we stay — selecting, contracting and governing the specialists who run it day to day, with one point of contact and one team accountable to you for the result.

We are not an agency, and we are not a strategy house. We are the partner in between: the people who have built consumer businesses in China, who know which local operators are genuinely good, and who will sit at your table for years rather than hand over a deck and leave.

The gap

Brands arrive in China without the pieces that make it work.

Global strategy firms

Give you PowerPoint, not a business.

A market-entry report is not a market entry. Somebody still has to choose the operators, sign the contracts and answer the phone in Shanghai.

Local agencies

Know the culture, not your operation.

Excellent at content, KOLs and campaigns. Rarely accountable for whether the brand actually makes money — or for anything outside their own scope.

Boutique studios

Sell PDFs, not partnerships.

A Chinese name and a beautiful deck, then a handover. Nobody's incentives survive the launch.

If you don't have local arms — we are.
