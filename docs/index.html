The idea has changed

You don't need to be in China. You need to understand it.

Five years ago the question was whether to set up in China. Now it's whether you understand a market that has moved further and faster than any other — in how people discover, judge and buy brands.

Chinese consumers shop inside livestreams, take advice from AI assistants, pay with their face, and expect a brand to answer on WeChat in minutes. Domestic brands have become formidable — faster, more digital, and often better at telling a Chinese story than the imports. The bar for a Western brand is higher. The cost of finding out where you stand is lower than it has ever been.

ArcOn Brands helps you understand first, then decide — and, if you go, we build and run it with you.

What's changed

China, 2020 → now

Then

  • Set up an entity, hire a team, open on Tmall
  • Search, banners and a KOL campaign
  • Foreign heritage was the story
  • Learn by spending — 18 months to a first read
  • A Chinese name was a translation task

Now

  • Test cross-border first — no entity, real demand data in weeks
  • Livestream commerce on Douyin; discovery on Xiaohongshu; AI assistants shaping what people ask for
  • Heritage still matters — but only when it's told in a Chinese idiom, against confident local brands
  • Learn by analysis — AI-assisted market reads before you commit a yuan
  • A Chinese name is brand strategy, and a race to the trademark office
