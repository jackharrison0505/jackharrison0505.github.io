How to enter China Talk to us
The question has changed

Not if. When, how, and with whom.

China is the largest consumer market on earth and still one of the hardest to read from outside. Brands that succeed there don't just export — they arrive with the right name, the right channels, the right partners and a plan measured in years, not seasons.

ArcOn Brands exists to make that arrival work. We are based in Hong Kong and Shanghai with a London office, we speak the language, and we have spent three decades between us building consumer businesses in the region.

Market entry strategy

In-depth market analysis and a detailed entry plan — channels, pricing, positioning and a four-year budget forecast — so the decision to go is made on evidence.
